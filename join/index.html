Five minutes, no money

Join

Joining means signing one line of text with a bitcoin wallet. It costs nothing, moves nothing, and commits you to no payment ever. Putting money behind it is a separate decision, taken later or never.

What you are putting your name to

You are signing the Supporters' Declaration — read it first if you have not. In short: supporters buying a stake in Liverpool Football Club, with nobody ever holding anybody else's money, and nothing ever paid out to any member.

The statement below carries the declaration's fingerprint. That is what stops anyone ever claiming you agreed to different words: your signature only matches this exact document.

Sign it with your wallet

Signing a message is not a transaction. It never touches the blockchain, costs no fee, and works on a wallet that has never held a penny. All it proves is that you hold the key.

Which wallet are you using?

One thing worth ten seconds of thought. The key you sign with is the key that would own your locks if you ever commit money. Use a wallet you intend to keep and have backed up — not something you will wipe next month. You can always register a second address later, but you would be a second member rather than the one you started as.

If the button above is broken, or you would simply rather not route it through us, use one of these and you lose nothing:

    

      

        Open a pull request
        
Add your record to registrations.json in the repository. Needs a GitHub account.

      

      

        Hand it to anybody who will publish it
        
A signature cannot be forged and needs nobody's permission to pass on. Post it anywhere, give it to a friend who uses GitHub, or send it to whoever is collecting. Nobody you pass it through can alter it without breaking it — that part is cryptography. What is not yet true is that no operator can keep you out: today there is one published register, merged by one person. Anyone can run their own, and until somebody does, that is a fact worth knowing rather than a promise we can make.
