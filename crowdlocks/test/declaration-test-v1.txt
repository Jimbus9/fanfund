THE TEST DECLARATION

This is a test. It exists only so that software can be tried with test money
on Bitcoin's test network (testnet4), and it means nothing.

Signing it does not make anybody a member of The Kop Fund or of anything else.
It is not the Supporters' Declaration, and a signature over this text can
never be counted as one: the two have different fingerprints.

Test bitcoin has no value. Nothing on this register is real money, and
nothing on it will ever be counted towards any real total.

Anyone may sign it, as often as they like, to see how joining works.
